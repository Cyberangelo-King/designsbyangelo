THE COST OF CONFORMITY

THE THOUGHT

An essay becomes a visual argument. The campaign translates conformity into repetition and independent judgement into a deliberate interruption. A strict grid establishes order; oversized type, cropped forms and a disruptive signal colour challenge it without sacrificing hierarchy.
