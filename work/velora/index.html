The standard of clean

THE THOUGHT

Cleanliness is often sold as a chore. VELORA reframes it as a daily ritual: calm, tactile and quietly desirable. The identity pairs a disciplined typographic system with botanical geometry and a restrained palette, giving everyday hygiene the visual language of considered care.
